# Stem-changing verbs in the present tense

Some verbs change the last vowel of their stem when it is stressed. The endings stay regular.

## The boot pattern

The change happens in yo, tú, él/ella/usted and ellos/ellas/ustedes - but **not** in nosotros and vosotros,
where the stress falls on the ending. Drawn as a conjugation table, the changed forms make the shape of a boot.

## e -> ie

querer (to want): quiero, quieres, quiere, queremos, queréis, quieren

Others: pensar, empezar, cerrar, entender, preferir, sentir, perder, comenzar, despertarse.

## o -> ue

poder (to be able): puedo, puedes, puede, podemos, podéis, pueden

Others: dormir, volver, encontrar, contar, recordar, costar, morir, almorzar.
**Jugar** is the only u -> ue verb: juego, juegas, juega, jugamos, jugáis, juegan.

## e -> i (only -ir verbs)

pedir (to ask for): pido, pides, pide, pedimos, pedís, piden

Others: servir, repetir, seguir, vestirse, decir (also irregular yo: digo).

## Watch out

- **Tener** and **venir** combine an e -> ie change with an irregular yo form: tengo, tienes...; vengo, vienes...
- The stem change only happens in the stressed syllable, so the infinitive and the nosotros form keep the
  original vowel: *pensamos, podemos, pedimos*.
- -ir stem-changers also change in the gerund: *durmiendo, pidiendo, sintiendo*. -ar and -er verbs do not:
  *pensando, volviendo*.
- Dictionaries often mark these verbs with the change in brackets: *dormir (ue)*, *pedir (i)*.
