# False friends (falsos amigos)

Words that look like English words but mean something else. These cause the most embarrassing mistakes.

| Spanish | Actually means | Not | Say instead |
|---------|----------------|-----|-------------|
| embarazada | pregnant | embarrassed | avergonzado/a |
| la librería | the bookshop | the library | la biblioteca |
| el éxito | success | exit | la salida |
| la carpeta | the folder | carpet | la alfombra |
| constipado/a | having a cold | constipated | estreñido/a |
| actual / actualmente | current / currently | actual / actually | en realidad |
| realizar | to carry out, to achieve | to realise | darse cuenta |
| asistir | to attend | to assist | ayudar |
| sensible | sensitive | sensible | sensato/a |
| la ropa | clothes | rope | la cuerda |
| molestar | to bother | to molest | acosar |
| la decepción | disappointment | deception | el engaño |
| el pariente | relative | parent | el padre, la madre, los padres |
| contestar | to answer | to contest | impugnar |
| largo/a | long | large | grande |
| el vaso | drinking glass | vase | el jarrón |

## Examples

- *Estoy embarazada* means "I'm pregnant". To say "I'm embarrassed", say *Tengo vergüenza* or *Me da vergüenza*.
- *La película fue un éxito* - the film was a success. The exit sign reads *Salida*.
- *Actualmente vivo en Madrid* - I currently live in Madrid. "Actually" is *en realidad* or *de hecho*.
- *Me di cuenta de que había olvidado las llaves* - I realised I had forgotten the keys.
- *Mis padres* are your parents; *mis parientes* are all your relatives.

## Good friends (cognates you can trust)

Many words ending in **-tion** become **-ción** (la nación, la información), **-ty** becomes **-dad**
(la universidad, la realidad, la electricidad), and **-ous** becomes **-oso** (famoso, delicioso).
